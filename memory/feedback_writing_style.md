Do not use em dashes (—) in any project descriptions, portfolio copy, or content written for the portfolio site.

**Why:** User explicitly called it out as "a clear sign that AI wrote the article." It breaks the natural voice.

**How to apply:** When writing `descriptionMd`, feature lists, short descriptions, or any user-facing copy for the portfolio, use colons, commas, or restructure the sentence instead.
